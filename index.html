Cutlist

Cutlist is a free cut list optimiser (optimizer) for plywood, MDF and other sheet goods. Enter your stock sheets, your parts and the kerf of your blade in millimetres, and it lays the parts out with guillotine cuts only: straight cuts from one edge to the other, the kind a table saw or a track saw makes.

  • A cutting order for every sheet, with the fence distance for each rip and crosscut, arranged to keep fence moves few.
  • Several candidate layouts for each job, with the waste and the offcuts worth keeping.
  • Grain kept along the sheet for any part that must not rotate.
  • A work mode for the workshop: tap each part as you cut it.
  • No account, no adverts and no limits. Everything runs in your browser, jobs are saved on your device, and a link can carry a whole job.
  • Open source under AGPL-3.0, at github.com/XDGFX/cutlist.
